Gold Loan Register - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Gold Loan Register is an offline app that helps a gold loan or pawn shop keep its loan records on the owner's own phone. This page explains what the app does with information.

Short version

The app does not collect your data and does not send your data anywhere. Everything you type or import stays on your phone. The developer cannot see it.

What is stored on your phone

The app stores the records you enter or import: party names and addresses, loan details, payments, daily tally figures, notes, and settings such as the shop name. This information is kept in the app's private storage on your phone only.

Files you create or choose

  • Backup files and CSV export files are saved to your phone's Downloads folder only when you ask the app to make them. You decide where they go afterwards.
  • To import data, you choose the files yourself using Android's file picker.
  • Party photos and ID card pictures are optional. The app reads and writes pictures only inside the one folder that you select with Android's folder picker. It does not look at your other photos or files.

Sharing

The app shares nothing by itself. If you tap a Share button (for example for a working slip), Android opens the apps you can share with, and the content goes only to the app you choose.

What the app does not do

  • It does not need an account or sign-in.
  • It does not show advertisements.
  • It does not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored inside it. Backup files, CSV files and pictures that you saved in your own folders stay there until you delete them yourself.

Keeping your data safe

Your records are only as safe as your phone. Please use a screen lock and keep your own backup copy. This is a small independent app and it comes without any guarantee.

Children

The app is made for shop owners and staff. It is not meant for children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated with a new date.
