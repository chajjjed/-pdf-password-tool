Privacy Policy for PDF Unlocker

Package: com.chajjed.pdfunlocker · Last updated: 7 October 2026

PDF Unlocker ("the app") is a small hobby project made by Narendra Chajjed. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

What the app does

You pick password-protected PDF files on your phone. The app tries the passwords you have saved in its password list. If none match, it asks you to type one. When a password works, the app creates an unlocked copy of the PDF (or, if you tick the option, replaces the original file).

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The developer does not receive your PDF files, your passwords, your name, your location, or any other data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Internet access

The app does not use the internet and contains no code that sends data anywhere. Everything happens on your phone.

Files and storage permission

The app asks for "All files access" on your phone only so that it can show the folders on your phone and let you choose PDF files. It reads the PDFs you choose and writes the unlocked copies in the same folder as the original. It does not open or read any other files.

Where your passwords are kept

Passwords you enter are saved in a list inside the app's private storage on your phone, so the app can reuse them next time. Please note: this list is not encrypted. Other apps cannot normally read the app's private storage, but if your phone is rooted, lost, or someone else has access to it, the saved passwords could be seen. You can view the saved passwords inside the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the list.

No guarantee

This app is made as a hobby and is provided "as is", without any promise of security, accuracy or fitness for any purpose. Please do not rely on it to protect sensitive passwords, and always keep your own backup of important PDF files before using the "overwrite original file" option. The developer is not responsible for any loss of files or data.

Use it lawfully

Use the app only on PDF files that belong to you or that you have permission to open.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
